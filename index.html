How it works

Track your learning

Change a topic's status from the table, use search and filters, or add your own topics. Your progress bar updates automatically.

Use it on any device

Local mode saves data only in this browser. For cross-device access, connect Supabase and create an account. Sign in with the same account on your other devices.

Cloud storage

Once Supabase is connected, your topics and progress are saved to your account and sync across devices.

GitHub Pages

GitHub Pages hosts the static website. Supabase stores your cloud data.
